Prioritized 30-day plan

The fastest path is to consolidate query-to-page relevance, then lift proven rank-11-20 clusters before investing in distant head terms.

Resolve “image to URL” cannibalization

Choose one canonical target for the head cluster. Point homepage copy, internal anchors, breadcrumbs, and related tool pages toward that target. Keep JPG-specific intent on /en/jpg-to-url/.

High impact

Push the GIF page from 14 into the top 10

Add exact-intent copy for “GIF to URL converter,” strengthen contextual internal links from the homepage and related converter pages, and verify title/H1/snippet alignment.

Fast win

Consolidate the create-a-link cluster

The article already holds ranks 15, 17, and 19. Expand the answer block, add a short step-by-step example, and link it from high-authority tool pages using varied but relevant anchors.

Fast win

Upgrade PNG-to-PDF authority

Do not split synonyms into thin pages. Build one definitive tool page covering plural uploads, merge order, privacy, file limits, and output quality; earn links to this URL.

Big upside

Protect the Reddit EXIF guide

Keep the page fresh, answer the two ranking phrasings directly, and add internal links to relevant privacy/metadata tools. It is the site's only current page-one proof.

Defend

Measure with Search Console alongside ETV

Use GSC clicks and impressions to validate whether modeled ETV gains convert into real visits. Keep DataForSEO for market visibility, difficulty, rank depth, and competitive opportunity.
